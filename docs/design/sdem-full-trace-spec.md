# The full trace — every record's chain from A1 to A5, direct and through its parent shapes

> **Status: approved by the owner (2026-11-22: "write spec and implement … it must show a full trace") — being built.** Extends the
> knowledge graph's trace (`sdem/graph.py`, KG-Q-…; `--follow-composition`, which already climbed from a part to its whole downstream).
> Made-up data only.

## 1. Why

A design's links between layers join specific records, and many records link only through a **parent shape in their own layer**: a
BIAN operation is *part of* its service domain, which an A1 process uses; an API operation is *part of* its interface, which is exposed
through an A5 node. A trace that follows only the links between layers stops at the operation — `sdem trace` of *Payment Order
Initiation: Retrieve* found nothing upstream — and the 3D view, which lights a record's chain when it is clicked, showed fragments: a
line from A2 to A4 with nothing to A1 or A5. The chain is in the design; the trace did not walk it.

## 2. Rules

| Id | Rule |
|---|---|
| **FT-01** | **A trace walks "part of" as well as the links between layers.** A part-whole link inside a layer (composition, aggregation — ArchiMate's structural links) is a hop the trace may take, shown as such (`PART_OF`, `HAS_PART`, `COMPOSES`) and marked structural — nothing to approve. The links between layers are followed as before: approved only (statuses as given). |
| **FT-02** | **Which hops, so the chain is whole and never a sibling's.** From every record reached, the trace climbs to its whole (an operation to its service group and domain; an API operation to its interface and component). It steps down into parts only from the record traced, and — upstream — from a record reached by a link between layers (whatever depends on an application's modules depends on the platform it runs on). It never steps down from a record it reached by climbing: tracing *Retrieve* reaches its domain, not the domain's other operations. |
| **FT-03** | **The full trace is the default.** `sdem trace` (one record, or a layer) gives the full trace; `--direct-only` gives the links between layers alone, as before. `--follow-composition` is still accepted and means the default. Impact (`sdem impact`) is unchanged by this spec. The static dashboard (`sdem dashboard`) shows the full trace too, as the command does. |
| **FT-04** | **A trace ends only where the design does.** Where no link continues — a record with no link to the next layer, directly or through a whole — the chain ends there; the design's gaps are said by `sdem status` and the GAPS view, never filled by the trace. |
| **FT-05** | **The 3D view and the 2D map light the same chain.** Clicking a record lights FT-02's chain, across every layer, with the part-whole links it passed through, so a highlighted line never stands alone without its chain to A1 and A5 where the design has one. (DESNRE `index.html`, DW-67.) |
| **FT-07** | **A BIAN business object is part of its domain's information** *(the owner, 2026-11-22: "it's missing up and down links")*: a control record's link to a business object (BIAN's B08 — the domain's Business Object Model) is a part-whole hop for the trace, so *Payment Transaction* climbs to *Payment Order Initiation* and the A1 processes that use it. |
| **FT-08** | **Realization, assignment and an event's triggering are walked too** *(the owner, 2026-11-22: "continue and I agree")*: the realised is the whole (a value stream stage and the processes that realise it; a capability and its processes), the assigned-from is the whole (a role and the processes it is assigned to; a node and its system software), and the behaviour is the whole of the event that triggers it or that it triggers. All ArchiMate 3.2's own links; how the trace walks them is SDEM's rule. Without them a stage, a role or an event reached nothing (ROLE-PAY-OFFICER in the bundled example). |
| **FT-09** | **A record of a layer that cuts across the design is traced through what it links to** *(the owner, 2026-11-22: "yes")*: a security control, threat or trust boundary is about the design records it protects, threatens, bounds or is enforced by — its trace is the full trace of each of them, up and down, as if that record were the one asked, and never on into another control or threat. Without it a control on a network zone reached A5 only; on the Payment example, with FT-10, 16 of 25 security records reach A1–A5, and the other 9 stop where the design does (consent data mapped to no BIAN business object; applications that record no data they use). |
| **FT-10** | **A path reaches the nodes it connects** *(the owner, 2026-11-22: "ok implement")*: a path's association with a node (ArchiMate 3.2 §10.2.4: a path is associated with the nodes it connects — not composition) is a hop for the trace; the path is the whole, its nodes its ends. A threat to the hand-off path now reaches the nodes, what runs on them and the business above; a node climbs to the paths it is on, never to their other ends. |
| **FT-11** | **What depends on a whole depends on its information** *(the owner, 2026-11-22: "agree implement", MIP-02 — rule gap R1 of the [integrity analysis](sdem-metamodel-integrity-analysis.md))*: downstream, from a record reached by a link or by climbing, the trace steps into the whole's **information** parts only — a BIAN control record, business object or field, a business object, a data entity or field — never its other parts. A process that uses a service domain reaches the domain's business object and, through its MAPPED_TO, the Data layer; an operation reaches its domain's information, not the domain's other operations. |
| **FT-12** | **Serving inside a layer is a dependency** *(R2)*: what a record is served by, it depends on — downstream from the consumer to the provider, and on what realises the provider; upstream from the provider to the consumer. An actor reaches the services and channels that serve it, and below. |
| **FT-13** | **BIAN's classification of a domain** *(R3)*: a service domain's association with its asset type or functional pattern makes the domain the whole — a classifier reaches what its domain reaches; a domain reached is not opened into its classifiers. |
| **FT-14** | **Two vertical extensions** *(G4, the owner 2026-11-22: "implement all 3")*: a record beside the stack linked to a record of *another* layer beside it (a recovery plan verified by an assurance check, a control protecting a recovery plan) — the trace goes on through the other record into the design records *it* is about only where the extension's link type declares `"trace": "through"`; otherwise it never goes on into another vertical record (FT-09's default). Each record once. Two ends in one vertical layer are that layer's own link kinds, as before. |
| **FT-15** | **A channel and the services it exposes** *(the owner, 2026-11-22)*: a business interface's association with a business service (ArchiMate 3.2 §8.2.3: an interface exposes the services associated with it) — the service is the whole, so a channel reaches what the services it exposes reach. Declared in `metamodel/a1-business.json` (`trace.rules`), not written into the trace (MID-08). |
| **FT-06** | **Proved**: on a made-up design, *Retrieve*'s full trace reaches the A1 process that uses its domain and the A5 node its API's interface is exposed through; it does not reach the domain's other operations; `--direct-only` gives the old answer; the existing composition traces keep their answers (module → whole → platform downstream; platform → modules upstream). |
